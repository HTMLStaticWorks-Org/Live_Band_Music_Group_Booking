The Stage Ethos

Real instruments. Zero backing tracks.

Founded in 2015 at Mercer Soundstage, Encore was born from a desire to bring the electricity and acoustic precision of arena touring acts to private events, galas, and destination celebrations.

We never assemble ad-hoc pickup bands on the night of your show. Every group on our roster is a fixed touring ensemble that rehearses together weekly, guided by a musical director who knows how to read a room in real time.

Every performance is backed by our in-house production crew. Our front-of-house engineers tune each line array to your room's exact geometry, which is the only way a twelve-piece brass band sounds both thunderous and crystal clear.

Stage Craft

Every note mixed live for your room

Whether you host 90 guests inside a limestone conservatory or 600 attendees on a waterfront terrace, our production scales transparently around the band.

Room-tuned line arrays

Our engineers model room reflections ahead of load-in so dance-floor punch never bleeds loudly into seated dinner tables.

DMX light shows

Warm candle-amber uplights soften cocktail sets before synchronized moving-head beams elevate late-night encores.

Seamless setlist pacing

Zero countdown silence or awkward laptop pauses — live horn swells and vinyl-mixed transitions carry energy straight to curfew.

What We Believe

Three rules we never bend

100% live musicianship

If you hear a four-part horn riff or a soaring vocal harmony, someone on stage is performing it live.

Production in-house

We never outsource audio or lighting. The engineers who calibrate soundcheck mix the final encore.

One show per night

No band ever double-books an evening. Load-in is unhurried, and the stage is yours until the lights come up.
